FAQ

Questions we get asked.

What is a UGC brand?

A UGC brand is a company that actively works with creators for user-generated content — reposting creator videos, running an ambassador program, or paying for content rights. That's the kind of brand GetMeBrands looks for.

How do you find brands running ads or engaging with creators?

A human checks for real signals on each brand: tagged creator content, a visible ambassador or UGC page, and an active social presence — not just any brand that happens to exist in a niche.

Do I need a large following to use this?

No minimum. Small and emerging brands are often the easiest to land, regardless of your follower count.
