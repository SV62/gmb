Three ways to slice a niche

Every list starts with a question you already have.

Pick the angle that matches your outreach motion, then narrow by niche, size, and platform.

▲

Actively advertising

Brands currently spending on Meta, Google or TikTok ads right now — a signal they have budget, are testing acquisition, and are worth a media-kit pitch or partnership offer.

◆

Social-first brands

Brands with a real, active audience on Instagram or TikTok — good targets for influencer collabs, affiliate offers, or organic-first partnership pitches.

●

Small & emerging

Early-stage brands without heavy ad spend or a big following yet — lower competition for your outreach, and often the fastest to say yes.
